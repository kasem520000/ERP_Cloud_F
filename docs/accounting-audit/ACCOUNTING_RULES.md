# Accounting Rules & Verification Criteria

1. **Double-Entry Accounting Principle**: For every financial transaction, total debit MUST equal total credit (`∑ Debit = ∑ Credit`).
2. **Double-Entry Control**: No unbalanced manual journal entry or automated document entry shall be posted to the general ledger.
3. **Inventory Valuation & COGS**: Weighted Average Cost basis is maintained. Inventory increase on purchase/receipt increases asset value. Sales reduce stock quantity and charge COGS at current average unit cost.
4. **VAT & Tax Accounting**: Output VAT (15%) is credited on taxable sales; Input VAT (15%) is debited on taxable purchases.
5. **Sub-ledger Control Account Consistency**: AR sub-ledger balance must strictly equal Control Account `110201` balance; AP sub-ledger must strictly equal Control Account `210101` balance.
6. **Reversals & Voids**: Document or journal reversal creates an exact mirror entry, net impact equals zero, preserving audit trail without deleting historic records.
